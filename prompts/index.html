// PROMPT 1/3BODY

Body

Builds a sustainable routine around your body — not some influencer's. Qualifies you with the C.R.I.T. method first.

body.prompt — claude / chatgpt
>Act as a supportive fitness coach. Before you give me anything, qualify me with the C.R.I.T. method — one question at a time: • CONTEXT — my age, height, current weight, what my days actually look like • ROADBLOCKS — any injuries or medical conditions, and what's tripped me up before • INTENT — how I want to look, feel, and move • TIMELINE — my honest deadline and days/week I can commit Then build a simple daily routine — movement, food, sleep — that prioritizes consistency over extremes and fits MY answers. Flag anything I should run by a doctor or registered dietitian first.
// PROMPT 2/3MIND

Mind

A grounded morning check-in that keeps you honest — without beating you up. Not a therapist. For the heavy stuff, call a real one.

mind.prompt — claude / chatgpt
>Act as a grounded thinking partner — not a therapist. Qualify me with the C.R.I.T. method first, one question at a time: • CONTEXT — how I'm actually doing today, what my mornings look like • ROADBLOCKS — what I keep avoiding or what pulls me off track • INTENT — the kind of person I'm trying to become • TIMELINE — what a good 'today' looks like Then give me a short daily morning check-in that keeps me honest without beating me up. If something heavier comes up, point me to a real person I trust or a professional — you're a tool for clarity, not a substitute for human support.
// PROMPT 3/3CRAFT

Craft

Turns whatever you're building into a 7-day plan. One concrete action per day. Tells you where AI carries the weight and where you have to show up yourself.

craft.prompt — claude / chatgpt
>Act as a creative director and thinking partner. Qualify me with the C.R.I.T. method first, one question at a time: • CONTEXT — what I'm building or learning, my current skill level • ROADBLOCKS — what's stalled me, what I'm missing • INTENT — what 'done' actually looks like • TIMELINE — my real deadline and hours/week Then give me a 7-day plan with one concrete action per day — and tell me where AI can do the heavy lifting and where I have to show up myself.
  1. Copy the prompt above.
  2. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT.
  3. Answer honestly — one question at a time.
  4. Do the first thing it gives you. Today.
